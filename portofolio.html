FTC 2024 Robot

This is my robotics team's 12.78 kg FTC robot for the INTO THE DEEP season, featuring a compact Mecanum drive base with lightweight, geometrically patterned side panels and polycarbonate shielding. It incorporates a horizontal extension mechanism powered by Axon Max servos on MISUMI SAR220 linear slides, a 360-degree rotating passive claw, and a vertical lift system utilizing 10 MISUMI SAR230 slides driven by dual GoBilda motors

Technologies Used:

  • CAD Program: OnShape
  • Programming Languages & Control: Kotlin
  • GoBilda 435/312 RPM motors
  • Axon Max & Mini servos
  • 3-wheel odometry

FTC 2025 Robot

This robot's name is JENIKA 2, it is the high-speed, flywheel-based shooting robot designed for the FTC DECODE season. Built on a low-profile, 435 RPM Mecanum chassis sitting just 5 mm off the ground for stability, the robot is engineered for rapid artifact collection, precise sorting, and automated high-velocity shooting.

Technologies Used

  • CAD Program: OnShape
  • Programming Languages & Control: Kotlin
  • Autonomous Trajectory & Movement
  • 435 RPM Mecanum drivetrain
  • GoBilda high-RPM motors

Arduino Smart Net

The Arduino Smart Net is an automated aquatic monitoring system designed to track fish activity and water conditions in real time. Powered by an Arduino microcontroller, the system uses non-intrusive counting sensors to tally fish as they pass through the net's opening, immediately displaying the count on an integrated LCD screen. To evaluate the aquatic environment, it incorporates a waterproof temperature sensor to measure water warmth and a salinity sensor to gauge salt concentration, providing a compact, all-in-one display of biological and environmental data.

Technologies Used

  • Arduino Uno
  • Waterproof DS18B20 temperature sensor
  • Salinity sensor
  • Ultrasound sensor
  • 16x2 LCD Display
